1. Introduction

Welcome to DocuEdit: PDF & Image Editor. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

DocuEdit provides document and image-related functionality, allowing users to work with PDF files and images directly on their Android devices.

Our application does not require users to create an account, register, sign in, or provide personal identity information to access its core features.

By downloading, installing, or using DocuEdit, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

We do not directly request or collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, home address, or account credentials through user registration or login.

B. Documents and User-Selected Files

When you select PDF documents or other files using the application's features, the selected content is accessed and processed locally on your device.

  • Selected PDF documents.
  • Selected images and photographs.
  • Document editing and processing inputs.
  • Files generated or exported by the application.

We do not upload your selected documents or images to our own servers or maintain copies of your personal files in our cloud storage.

C. Information Collected by Advertising Services

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device, advertising, and technical information when advertisements are displayed in the application. Details are explained in Section 7.

D. Analytics

DocuEdit does not integrate Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or a separate third-party analytics service for collecting user activity or application analytics.

However, Google Mobile Ads SDK may process certain interaction and diagnostic information for advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

3. PDF & Document Processing

DocuEdit is designed to process supported PDF documents directly on your Android device.

Depending on the features you use, the application may allow you to:

  • Open and view PDF documents.
  • Edit or modify supported document content.
  • Convert or process supported document formats.
  • Perform document-related operations.
  • Export processed documents to a location selected by you.

The documents you select are processed locally by the application. We do not operate a proprietary document-processing server or cloud-based file storage service.

Your original documents remain under your control. Exported files are saved to the destination selected by you through the Android file system.

4. Image Access & Processing

DocuEdit allows users to select and process images using Android's supported system file-selection mechanisms.

Android Photo Picker

Where supported, the application uses Android's Photo Picker to allow you to select specific images or photographs.

This allows you to choose individual photos without granting the application unrestricted access to your entire photo gallery.

Storage Access Framework (SAF)

The application may also use Android's Storage Access Framework to allow you to browse, select, open, and save supported files through the system-provided file picker.

Access is limited to the files and locations you select or authorize through Android's system interfaces.

Selected images are processed locally and are not uploaded to our own servers.

5. Android Permissions

DocuEdit uses Android system capabilities and permissions necessary to provide its features.

A. Internet Permission

Internet access is used to allow Google AdMob to request and display advertisements.

B. Photo Picker Access

The Android Photo Picker allows users to select individual photos and images for processing.

C. Storage Access Framework

The Storage Access Framework allows users to select input files and choose export destinations using Android's built-in file selection interface.

DocuEdit does not require unrestricted access to the user's entire photo gallery merely to select individual files through these interfaces.

The exact permissions and access capabilities may vary depending on the Android version, device, and features being used.

6. How We Use Information

Information accessed or processed by the application is used for the following purposes:

  • Document Processing: To open, edit, process, and export PDF documents.
  • Image Processing: To allow users to select and work with images.
  • File Management: To read selected files and save exported files to user-selected locations.
  • Advertising: To display advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Service Reliability: To maintain the normal operation of the application's existing features.

We do not sell your personal documents, photographs, or personal information.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal documents or photographs.

10. Data Storage, Security & Retention

A. Local File Storage

DocuEdit processes selected documents and images locally on your device. Exported files are saved to the location selected by you.

B. No Account or Cloud Storage

The application does not require user registration, login, or account creation. We do not operate our own cloud storage or document synchronization servers.

C. Data Retention

Files saved by you remain at their selected storage locations until you delete them or otherwise manage them through your device.

Any temporary files or application data, where created, may remain within the application's local storage until removed by the application, cleared through Android Settings, or removed upon uninstallation, subject to Android system behavior.

D. Security

Android's application sandboxing and operating system security mechanisms help protect application-private data. However, no electronic storage or processing method can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

DocuEdit is a general-purpose PDF and image utility application and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through account registration or login mechanisms.

Third-party advertising services may have their own requirements and restrictions regarding children's privacy and age-appropriate advertising.

12. Your Privacy Choices

You can:

  • Select only the documents and images you want to process.
  • Choose where your exported files are saved.
  • Delete exported files through your device's file manager.
  • Manage advertising preferences through supported Google and Android privacy settings.
  • Clear local application data through Android Settings.
  • Uninstall the application at any time.

Since we do not maintain user accounts or store your documents on our own servers, there is no account-based deletion process required.

13. Offline Functionality

DocuEdit is designed to perform its core document and image-processing operations locally on your device.

Certain internet-dependent services, particularly Google AdMob advertisements, require an active internet connection.

Advertisement availability may be affected when your device is offline. This does not mean your personal documents are uploaded to a cloud server.
