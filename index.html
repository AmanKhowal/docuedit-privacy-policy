Privacy Policy for DocuEdit

Last updated: November 09, 2026

DocuEdit ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect very limited information:

  • AdMob Advertising: We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID) to serve personalized ads based on their policies.

2. Use of Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob.

3. Data Storage

All your documents and edited files are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your personal files or documents.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates Google AdMob. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle data.
